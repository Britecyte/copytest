From mission to platform.

Britecyte was born in 2020 out of a quiet laboratory, a global shutdown, and an unfinished journey.

When the COVID-19 pandemic forced the world to a standstill, Founder and Chief Scientific Officer Dr. Alla Danilkovitch saw an unexpected opportunity. Renting a single laboratory bench, she channeled the global silence into absolute, singular focus. Her mission? To solve complex, unmet medical needs through biocompatible, allogeneic human cell and tissue-based therapies.

By 2022, that intense isolation and time spent in the lab yielded a breakthrough: the successful development of Britecyte’s proprietary allogeneic adipose technology. With initial funding secured, Samson Tom joined as President and CEO to help build a team and transform this scientific breakthrough into a commercial reality.

But Britecyte’s roots run much deeper than 2020. For decades, the promise of regenerative medicine has captivated scientists. Alla, Sam, and early colleagues spent years at Osiris Therapeutics, helping develop and launch first-in-class products like Osteocel®, Cartiform®, Grafix®, and Stravix®. Their team also led the development of Prochymal® (later Ryoncil®), the world’s first approved stem cell therapy.

Today, Britecyte carries that legacy forward by developing therapies designed to restore structure, function, and quality of life.

2020—A Mission Begins. Britecyte is founded with a belief that the future of medicine is not only replacing what is missing, but restoring what has been lost.
2022—Turning Vision into Innovation. Britecyte develops its proprietary adipose tissue technology platform, opening new possibilities for restoring structure and function across clinical applications.
2023—From Research to Patients. Liposana® becomes Britecyte's first commercial product, supporting clinicians treating fat pad loss and soft tissue deficiencies in the foot.
2024—Expanding into Aesthetics. Lipoderma® is introduced to restore the structural tissue beneath the skin that helps maintain soft, natural facial contours.
2025—Continuing to Advance. Clinical research expands with FDA IND clearance and launch of an MSCRF-supported osteoarthritis clinical trial.
2026—Platform Technology Patented. The U.S. Patent and Trademark Office awards Britecyte its first two patents covering the company's adipose tissue platform technology.

A breakthrough in Biorestoration science.

A new era of restoration begins with technology that makes native adipose tissue available when and where it is needed.

  1. 01

    Native Structure & Function Preserved

    Britecyte's proprietary processing technology preserves the native architecture of adipose tissue, including intact adipocytes and extracellular matrix.

  2. 02

    Immunogenic Components Removed

    The technology selectively removes immunogenic components that have historically limited adipose tissue to autologous use.

  3. 03

    Ready When You Need It

    Stable refrigerated storage simplifies logistics while providing an off-the-shelf solution when patients need treatment.

  4. 04

    Designed for Minimally Invasive Delivery

    The preserved tissue is formulated for minimally invasive implantation using standard cannulas or needles, allowing physicians to restore lost structure through a familiar clinical workflow.
